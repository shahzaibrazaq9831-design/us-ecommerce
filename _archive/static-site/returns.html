Refund & Returns Policy

7-day returns on eligible unstitched dresses

7-Day Return Window: We offer a clear and fair returns process. Eligible unstitched dresses — including sale items — may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Some conditions apply.

What Can Be Returned?

  • Unstitched dresses in their original, unworn condition
  • Items with all original tags and packaging intact
  • Items that have not been altered, washed, or damaged
  • Regular-priced and sale items alike

What Cannot Be Returned?

  • Custom-stitched or altered garments made to your measurements
  • Items that have been worn, washed, or show signs of use
  • Accessories and undergarments for hygiene reasons
  • Items marked as "final sale" at the time of purchase

How to Start a Return

  • Contact our support team within 7 days of delivery at support@premiumpakistanidresses.com
  • Include your order number and reason for return
  • Our team will guide you through the return shipping process
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days after we receive and inspect the item

Exchanges

We're happy to help with exchanges for size or style. Exchanges are subject to availability. Contact our support team and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.
