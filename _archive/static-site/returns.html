Refund & Returns Policy

Cancellations, defective items and refunds

Returns for faulty or damaged items: If an item arrives defective or damaged, contact us with your order number and photos of the issue. After we verify it, we will offer a refund or a replacement.

Cancelling your order

Changed your mind right after ordering? Contact us within 12 hours of placing your order and we will cancel it before it ships. Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled.

No change-of-mind returns or exchanges

Because our pieces are made to order, we do not offer change-of-mind returns, refunds or exchanges. Please double-check your size, colour and details before placing your order — see our Size Chart or contact us if you need help choosing.

How to report a faulty or damaged item

  • Contact our support team as soon as you notice the issue at support@premiumpakistanidresses.com
  • Include your order number and photos of the issue
  • After we verify it, we will offer a refund or a replacement
  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days after we receive and inspect the item

Return shipping

If you do need to return an approved faulty or damaged item, the buyer pays the return shipping cost. We recommend using a tracked service.

Wrong address

We are not responsible for non-delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete shipping address entered at checkout, and no refund can be issued in that case. Please contact us as soon as you notice an address mistake — if the parcel has not shipped yet, we can update it.
