Shipping Policy

Worldwide delivery information and timelines

Shipping Overview

We are pleased to offer free standard worldwide delivery on all prepaid orders. Your order is carefully packed and shipped from our facility to ensure it arrives in perfect condition.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1-3 business days. Custom-stitched orders may require additional time (up to 7 business days). You will receive a confirmation email with tracking details once your order ships.

Customs & Import Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the customer. These charges are not included in our product prices or shipping costs.
